# Jason Mayo

Creative developer based in Manchester, UK. Co-founder of [MadeByShape](https://madebyshape.co.uk). Self-taught developer with a graphic design background.

On the side I make apps and [Craft CMS plugins](https://plugins.craftcms.com/developer/545) under [ByMayo](https://bymayo.co.uk).

- [**Every Creative**](https://everycreativejobs.com) — A hiring platform for creative studios and agencies
- [**Tap Tourist**](https://taptourist.co.uk) — A guide to the best craft beer spots in Manchester
